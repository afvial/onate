Translation (EN)

Before we take up the division of price, we must define what price is in general, although I have not found its definition in any Doctor. Price is used both in the broadest sense and in the strict sense: taken in the broadest sense, it transcends our purpose and the subject matter of the sales we are dealing with, and is found in all things, both material and spiritual, and is nothing other than the estimation of each thing. This estimation is the right judgment of things, by which, comparing some with others, and as it were weighing them on the faithful balance of our judgment, one against another, we either rank them lower, or rank them higher, or judge them equal, according to the merit and worth of each thing.
